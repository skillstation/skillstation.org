Data Consent & Terms of Enquiry

How Skill Station Academy collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you submit a course enquiry.

Effective Date: November 2026 Applicable Domain: skillstation.org

1. Overview & Explicit Consent

By checking the consent box and submitting an enquiry form on Skill Station Academy (skillstation.org), you voluntarily provide your personal information and grant explicit consent for us to collect, process, store, and use that data strictly for academic counselling, course administration, and admission support.

If the applicant is a minor (under 18 years of age, such as a school student), submission of the form confirms that a parent or legal guardian has reviewed and consented to these terms on the student's behalf.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit our Student Course Enquiry Form, we collect only the details necessary to assist you effectively:

  • Identity Information: Student's full name.
  • Academic & Professional Profile: Current level (School, College, or Working Professional), program category, and specific course of interest.
  • Contact Details: Parent or student email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and preferred mode of communication.
  • Enquiry Preferences: Planned joining timeline and optional notes regarding preferred batch timings or mode of learning.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is used exclusively for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To respond to your course enquiry and share syllabus details, batch schedules, and fee structures.
  • To contact you via your selected communication preference (WhatsApp, Phone Call, or Email).
  • To send automated enquiry confirmation receipts and essential admission updates.
  • To improve our academic offerings and student support experience through aggregated, non-identifiable internal records.

4. Communication Consent

By submitting your contact information, you authorize Skill Station Academy's academic counsellors to reach out to you via phone call, SMS, WhatsApp, or email regarding your enquiry—even if your phone number is registered under a Do-Not-Disturb (DND) or Do-Not-Call (DNC) registry—strictly in connection with the educational programmes you expressed interest in.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. Access to your enquiry details is restricted to authorized academic counsellors and administrative staff at Skill Station Academy. We rely on trusted, access-controlled cloud infrastructure (such as Google Workspace) solely to record enquiries and deliver confirmation emails.

6. Data Retention & Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Enquiry records are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill admission counselling and academic record-keeping obligations, or until you request deletion.

7. Your Rights (Access, Correction & Withdrawal)

In accordance with applicable data protection laws (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to:

  • Access & Review: Request a summary of the personal data we hold from your enquiry.
  • Correction: Request updates or corrections to inaccurate contact or academic details.
  • Withdraw Consent & Erasure: Withdraw your consent at any time and request permanent deletion of your enquiry data from our records.
